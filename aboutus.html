INNOVATING THE FUTURE OF TECHNOLOGY

Technology is the practical application of scientific knowledge, tools, techniques, and systems to solve problems, achieve goals, or manipulate the human environment. It encompasses both tangible hardware (machines, gadgets) and intangible software/methods (processes, software) created by humans to improve efficiency, productivity, and quality of life.

Key Aspects of Technology Purpose: The primary goal is to satisfy human needs and solve problems, such as communication, transportation, manufacturing, or healthcare. Application of Knowledge: Technology stems from scientific research and organized knowledge to create new tools or methods. Broad Definition: It includes simple, traditional tools (e.g., stone tools, wheels) as well as complex, modern systems (e.g., computers, internet, AI). Continuous Evolution: Technology is constantly changing and advancing.
