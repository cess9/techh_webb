  • About Us

Apple Music is a subscription-based streaming service offering over 100 million songs, ad-free, with high-quality lossless and Spatial Audio options. It allows offline listening, live radio, and integrates with Apple devices (iOS, macOS, HomePod) plus Android, Windows, and smart TVs.

•Key Features of Apple Music:
•Massive Library: Access to over 100 million songs and curated playlists.

•Audio Quality: Offers lossless audio and Spatial Audio with Dolby Atmos for immersive sound.
•Offline Listening: Users can download songs, albums, and playlists to play without an internet connection.

•Live Radio: Features 24/7 live radio stations, including Apple Music 1, Hits, and Country.
•Integration: Deeply integrated into the Apple ecosystem, including Siri, Apple Watch, and CarPlay.

•Music Library Management: Combines streamed music, Apple Music content, and personal libraries (previously bought on iTunes) in one place.

•Lyrics & Translations: Real-time synchronized lyrics with live translation features.

•Subscription Plans:
•Individual: Standard plan (approx. ₱139/month or $10.99/month).
