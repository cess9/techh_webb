Fitness equipment includes devices designed to improve physical health through cardiovascular exercise, strength training, flexibility, and recovery. Key categories include cardio machines (treadmills, ellipticals, bikes) for endurance, free weights (dumbbells, barbells) and selectorized machines for muscle strengthening, and functional tools (bands, balls) for versatility. Any device or apparatus that can be used to enhance your physical activity, health, and fitness. There are many types of fitness equipment available in the market, each with its own purpose, benefits, and drawbacks. In this article, we will review some of the most common fitness equipment and their uses.

Treadmill

treadmill is a device generally used for walking, running, or climbing while staying in the same place. Treadmills were introduced before the development of powered machines to harness the power of animals or humans to do work, often a type of mill operated by a person or animal treading the steps of a treadwheel to grind grain. In later times, treadmills were used as punishment devices for people sentenced to hard labour in prisons. The terms treadmill and treadwheel were used interchangeably for the power and punishment mechanisms.

Recumbent (fitness bike)

Recumbent fitness bike is a low-impact, stationary cardio machine featuring a reclined, seated position with a large backrest, designed to reduce strain on the back, neck, and joints. They offer a comfortable, stable workout with horizontal pedaling, making them ideal for rehabilitation and consistent, long-duration exercise.
