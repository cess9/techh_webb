Amazon Prime Video, known simply as Prime Video, is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming television service owned by Amazon. The service primarily distributes films and television series produced or co-produced by Amazon MGM Studios or licensed to Amazon, as Amazon Originals, with the service also hosting content from other providers, content add-ons, live sporting events, and video rental and purchasing services. Prime Video is offered both as a stand-alone service and as part of Amazon's Prime subscription. Amazon Prime Video is the second-most-subscribed video on demand streaming media service after Netflix. The service has around 205 million paid memberships worldwide.

Key Features and details:

Amazon has rebranded its experience, splitting the service into a "Core" benefit and a new "Ultra" tier to compete with high-end streaming quality.
Prime Video Ultra: As of April 2026, the previous "Ad-Free" add-on has been replaced by Prime Video Ultra. It is the only way to access 4K/UHD, Dolby Atmos, and 100 offline downloads.
The "Sports Hub": Amazon is now a major sports destination, holding exclusive rights to Thursday Night Football, NASCAR, and the 90th Masters Tournament (featuring advanced "Inside Amen Corner" analytics).
X-Ray & Shop the Show: Enhanced metadata (X-Ray) now allows users to buy outfits or products seen on screen directly through their Amazon account while the show is paused.
Channels: Users can subscribe to Max, Paramount+, or MGM+ directly within the Prime Video app, keeping all billing and viewing in one place.
Included with Prime: $14.99/mo or $139/year. Includes HD/HDR streaming with ads.

Prime Video Ultra (Add-on): +$4.99/mo. Removes ads and unlocks 4K, Dolby Atmos, and 5 concurrent streams.

Standalone Video: For non-Prime members, the service is available as a monthly subscription, but usually lacks the shipping and music perks.
