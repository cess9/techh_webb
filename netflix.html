  • About Us

Netflix is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming service. The service primarily distributes original and acquired films and television shows from various genres. It is available internationally in multiple languages. Netflix is undoubtedly the most important name in streaming. When it introduced an online streaming service in 2007, the company—which began as a mail-order DVD service in the late 1990s—completely disrupted the traditional media industry and is still the largest player in the market today.

•Key Features and Details:
•Streaming Content: Access to a vast library of entertainment including Netflix Originals, movies, and TV series.

•Cost & Plans: Pricing ranges from US$8.99 to US$26.99/month, allowing users to choose plans based on resolution (up to 4K) and number of devices.

•Device Compatibility: Compatible with smart TVs, game consoles, streaming media players, smartphones, tablets, and computers.
