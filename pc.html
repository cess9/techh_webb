A personal computer (PC) is a digital, multi-purpose device designed for individual use in homes, schools, or offices. It includes essential hardware—CPU, memory, storage, and a display—operating together to perform tasks like word processing, internet browsing, and gaming. PCs can be desktop units or portable laptops.

Desktop

A desktop computer is a stationary personal computer designed for regular use at a fixed location, such as on or under a desk. It typically features separate components—a computer tower (containing the CPU, motherboard, and storage), monitor, keyboard, and mouse—offering higher performance, modularity, and easier upgrades compared to portable laptops.

Netbooks

Netbooks are small, lightweight, and budget-friendly laptops designed primarily for basic internet browsing, document editing, and cloud-based tasks. Typically featuring 10- to 12-inch screens, these ultra-portable devices prioritize mobility over high performance. While popular in the late 2000s, modern, more powerful lightweight laptops have largely replaced them.

All in one Laptop

An all-in-one (AIO) desktop is a compact computer that integrates the monitor, CPU, motherboard, and speakers into a single unit, eliminating the traditional tower for a clutter-free, space-saving design. Ideal for modern, minimalist setups, they offer easy setup, often feature touchscreens, and use laptop-grade components suitable for daily tasks, home offices, and media consumption.
