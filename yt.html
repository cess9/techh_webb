YouTube Music is a dedicated music streaming service developed by YouTube (a Google company) that offers over 100 million songs, music videos, live performances, and podcasts. It competes with Spotify and Apple Music, providing personalized playlists, audio-only modes, and offline listening, leveraging YouTube's massive library to include rare content not found on other platforms.

•Key Features and Aspects of YouTube Music:

•Content Catalog: It goes beyond standard audio streaming, offering official albums, singles, remixes, live performances, and music videos.

•Subscription Options:
•Free (Ad-supported):

•Users can listen to music, view videos, and get recommendations, but it requires the app to remain open (no background play).

•YouTube Music Premium: A paid subscription offering ad-free listening, background play (music continues when using other apps), and offline downloads.

•Integration with YouTube Premium: A YouTube Music Premium subscription is often included with a full YouTube Premium subscription, which removes ads from all YouTube videos and includes downloads.

•Personalization & Discovery: Features personalized mixes, radios, and a "Samples" tab for discovering new music quickly.

•Search Features: Uses Google technology to allow users to search for songs based on lyrics or descriptions.

•Accessibility: Accessible via web browser, dedicated desktop apps, and mobile apps for Android and iOS, as well as smart speakers.
