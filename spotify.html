  • About Us

Spotify is a popular digital music, podcast, and video streaming service offering access to over 100 million songs and 7 million podcast titles, founded in 2006 in Sweden. It operates on a freemium model (ads-supported free service vs. paid, ad-free, high-quality audio premium) on devices like smartphones, computers, and smart speakers.

•Key Features of Spotify:
•Streaming & Access: Access over 100 million songs and 7 million podcasts from labels and creators worldwide.

•Freemium Model: •Free: Ad-supported, limited skips, shuffle-only on mobile, says YouTube video.

•Premium: Ad-free, offline listening (downloading), higher quality audio, and on-demand song skipping, says YouTube video.
